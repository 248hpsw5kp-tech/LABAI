
2026-02-19 02:33:47.481848 - To affordably extract DNA from E. coli K-12 using Taq polymerase:

1. Grow a culture of E. coli to mid-log phase (OD600 around 0.4) in LB broth supplemented with appropriate antibiotics if required, like ampicillin or kanamycin for selection markers.
2. Centrifuge the bacterial suspension and discard the supernatant to remove cell debris. Keep only the pellet at a low temperature (4°C) until all centrifugation steps are complete.
3. Resuspend the pellet in cold TE buffer containing Tris-HCl, pH 8.0 with EDTA as a chelating agent for Mg2+ to prevent amplification by existing DNA/RNA (e.g., use Taq_TE).
4. Add lysozyme and lyticase or perform mechanical disruption using a French press or beadbeater in TE buffer, incubate at 37°C for about an hour to break the cell walls efficiently without harsh conditions that could degrade DNA quality.
5. Perform alkaline lysis: Mix bacterial pellet with TE buffer and add Tris-Cl (pH ~10) containing SDS, phenol red as a pH indicator for about 3 min at room temperature; centrifuge to separate into two phases—cell debris/DNA stay in the supernatant while organic layer forms.
6. Collect both layers carefully using pipettes and transfer them back to TE buffer containing RNase (10U per sample) for a few minutes on ice, remove proteins from DNA by centrifugation; combine two aliquots with phenol/chloroform:isoamyl alcohol (25:144:1), vortex briefly and spin down phases to separate.
7. Transfer the desired quantity of the aqueous layer directly into another tube containing neutralization buffer, which usually consists of 0.03M EDTA in TE pH 8, mix gently on ice; precipitate DNA by adding cold ethanol or isopropanol (better yield) and incubate at -20°C for a couple hours to overnight until cleaned up from enzymes/proteins.
8. Centrifuge the sample again, collecting only supernatant containing pure DNA pellet after removing precipitated ethanol or isopropanol by pipetting off gently and discarding; resuspend in TE buffer for storage at 4°C until needed for PCR with Taq polymerase.
9. To verify successful extraction, run the sample on an agarose gel electrophoresis alongside a DNA marker or cut genomic E. coli K-12 DNAs as controls before use in PCR reactions to confirm amplicon size and yield using Taq polymerase due to its optimal activity at higher temperatures, like 72°C during extension step of the reaction.
